Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

"Bizarre Planet Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Bizarre Planet YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's authorization, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's own channel and to read that channel's own video statistics. It does not access any other user's data.

What it stores

It stores only the authorization token needed to act on the owner's channel, in a private location controlled by the owner. It collects no personal data from viewers and shares no data with third parties.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app's use of Google data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy.

YouTube's own terms apply: YouTube Terms of Service, Google Privacy Policy.
